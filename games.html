Block Builder

Build 1,000+ 3D voxel worlds, collect 18 animated voxel pets, customize 11 Skyblock islands, and race 1v1 against real player ghosts!

Game Page & Privacy Policy

Block Builder (com.TwentyThreeSteps.BlockBuilder) is a 3D voxel building puzzle & competitive ghost-racing game published by Pink Hornet (23-Steps). Follow the blueprint layer by layer in Guided Mode, test your spatial recall in Memory Mode, and race live 1v1 against recorded player ghosts.

Features

  • 1,000+ handcrafted 3D voxel levels
  • 18 collectible animated voxel pets (Dragon, Panda, Fox, Penguin, Axolotl & more)
  • 11 custom Skyblock island themes & 7 dynamic skies
  • Guided Blueprint Mode & Brain-Training Memory Mode
  • Live 1v1 Ranked Ghost Races & global leaderboards
Platforms: Android
